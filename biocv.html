Bio / CV

I am a Mathematician at the National Institute of Standards and Technology. My research focuses on the applied analysis and geometry of soft, fluid, and active matter. Prior to my current appointment, I was a Flatiron Research Fellow at the Center for Computational Biology at the Flatiron Institute with Prof. Michael Shelley. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Brown University under the supervision of Profs. Thomas Powers (Engineering & Physics) and Robert Pelcovits (Physics), and I previously studied physics and mathematics at the Georgia Institute of Technology. Download my CV.

In addition, I currently serve as the Gaithersburg Co-Chair for the ACMD Seminar Series. To invite someone to give a talk as part of this series, please contact me.

Before I became a researcher, I briefly worked as a teacher, a tutor, and an editor. My hobbies include long-distance running, paper folding, and yoga/meditation. I am fluent in Mandarin Chinese and study to achieve Japanese fluency in my free time.
